About the Council

Where Ideas Meet Industry

The Industry & Innovation Council (IIC) at SRMIST Ramapuram is established under the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC) to systematically foster the culture of innovation, startups, and entrepreneurship among students and faculty.

The council serves as the central hub for coordinating all corporate alliances, R&D consortia, and industry-sponsored Centres of Excellence across the campus — translating academic breakthroughs into commercial solutions.

Our Vision

To build a thriving innovation ecosystem at SRMIST Ramapuram that empowers students and faculty to become industry-ready innovators, entrepreneurs, and leaders who create real-world impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from CTOs, HR heads, and research directors exploring institutional collaboration

Our streamlined vetting process typically completes within 10 to 14 business days. Following an initial alignment meeting, our legal and council committee approves the standard bilateral template with mutual scope agreements.

Companies can engage through talent hiring and internships, establishing a sponsored Centre of Excellence (CoE), joint patent and R&D consultancy, sponsoring hackathons, or co-designing specialized curriculum electives.

IP rights are defined clearly in the MoU scope agreement prior to project initiation. Collaborations can follow joint ownership models or assign proprietary rights to the industry sponsor according to grant provisions.

Standard academic MoUs focusing on internships, guest lectures, and placement scouting involve zero financial commitments. Financial arrangements apply only to sponsored equipment laboratories, funded R&D grants, or specialized corporate scholarships.
